# Privacy

These are intended requirements for future implementation. The current scaffold does not collect or store personal data.

- Process study-session data locally.
- Make camera and activity collection optional and visible to the user.
- Keep camera frames transient; store derived observations and session summaries rather than recordings.
- Do not collect typed text, clipboard contents, or full browsing URLs.
- Stop collection when a session ends or tracking is paused.
- Provide controls to review, export, and delete session data.
- Present focus classifications as estimates and preserve unknown periods.
